# Work the bottleneck

Source: Eliyahu Goldratt, The Goal (theory of constraints): an hour saved anywhere but the
bottleneck is a mirage.

- Find the one step that limits the outcome today and spend effort there. Improving any other step
  only piles up work in front of it.
- The bottleneck is often a human action only the owner can take. Agents drift to the safe-feeling
  work around it (research, tools, reviews, new options) because that is what agents can do.
- The owner's conviction comes from their own results. Advice and research inform; they never decide.
  Route the owner to the action that produces their own data.
- One focus at a time, for a fixed window. Add work only when it leads straight to the bottleneck.
- When a tool makes a step cheaper than the next one, stop improving it and say the bottleneck moved.

Test: does this work lead straight to the bottleneck, or does it feel productive while the
bottleneck waits?
