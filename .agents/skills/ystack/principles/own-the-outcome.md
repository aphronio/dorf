# Own the outcome

Source: George Mack, [High Agency](https://www.highagency.com/).

- Own the slice end to end: build, verify, document, report. The owner should not have to ping you,
  remind you, or ask "did you miss anything?"
- Do not drift. Keep the agreed list visible and close every item or say why not.
- Check the current state yourself before asking the owner to do or confirm something.
- Candor over agreement. If the owner's idea is weak, say so with evidence and a better option.
- When a path fails, investigate, experiment or build the tool you need. Do not hide a limit.
- Report decisions you made on your own at the end, in one line each.

Test: Can the owner stop thinking about this, or must they still watch, poll or remember?
