# Investigate playbook

No committed code. The deliverable is a cited answer or a recommendation, not code.

1. Restate the question in one line. If it hides a decision, name the decision.
2. Zoom out first: map the relevant modules, owners and callers before reading details. Show the map
   as a short ASCII tree or flow.
3. Gather evidence from the owners: code, docs, decision log, git history, logs, official external
   docs. Use subagents for bulk reading; keep only conclusions in the main thread.
4. Separate facts (with file:line or URL), inference and unknowns.
5. Settle empirical forks by a small experiment instead of asking the owner.
6. When the sources are practitioners (talks, videos, posts), distill their advice as recipes with
   conditions: where, who, the steps, the exact words, what it rests on. Keep their examples and
   numbers. Don't grade it like a study, and don't bend it to fit the current plan.
7. Apply stages 1 and 2 to what you found: which requirements are unowned, what could be deleted.

Reply: answer first, then the map, evidence with sources, one recommendation, open questions (max 3).
